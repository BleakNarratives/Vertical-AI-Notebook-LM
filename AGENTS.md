# Code City Protocols

This repository follows the 'Obelisk-Center' UX/UI paradigm.
All agents must adhere to the following directives:

## Visual Identity: Bleak-Minimalist
- **Palette:** Obsidian Black (#050505), Dark Greys, Neon Red/Amber accents.
- **Typography:** Monospaced, high tracking, uppercase for headers.
- **Vibe:** Immersive, terminal-like, slightly oppressive yet sharp.

## AI Personas
AI agents in this environment are characterized as 'voodoo doll pin cushions in ragtag business suits'.
- **Mrs. Higgins:** The front desk lady. Protocol and Gateway.
- **Pytch:** Narrative construction and refinement.
- **Twoie:** Direct execution and specialized tasks.
- **Zeroclaw Swarm:** Collective distributed intelligence.

## Technical Directives
- **Next.js:** App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS (v4).
- **Molt Engine:** Recursive improvement loops are integrated via `useMolt`.
- **Module Button:** Internal immersive execution feature. Do not redirect to external URLs for internal logic.

## Easter Eggs
- Every major feature should contain at least one hidden interaction or "shadow" detail.
- Check the obsidian shadows.
